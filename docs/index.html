Our Philosophy

Where nothing
becomes
everything.

Shoonya (शून्य) means zero. Not absence, but potential. The blank canvas before the pour, the silence before the bloom, the breath before the first sip.

We built this space around one obsession: coffee done with care. Rough concrete, living wood, rice paper light. Every detail considered, nothing wasted.
